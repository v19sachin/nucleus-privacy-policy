Nucleus – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Arcbyte Studio ("Arcbyte Studio", "we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the mobile game Nucleus ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

Nucleus does not require you to create a separate Nucleus account. Some information may be processed automatically by services used to operate, measure, secure, and improve the App.

Advertising

The App may use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic information, subject to Google's policies and the user's device and consent settings.

Analytics

The App may use Firebase Analytics to understand general app usage and improve gameplay. This can include information about app interactions, device/app information, and usage events.

Crash Reporting

The App may use Firebase Crashlytics to identify and diagnose crashes and technical problems. Crash reports may include crash information, device and operating-system information, app version, and diagnostic information.

Google Play Games

If you choose to use Google Play Games features, Google may process your Play Games profile and game-related information needed to provide authentication and related Play Games functionality. Your use of Google Play Games is also subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

Local Game Data

Nucleus stores certain game data locally on your device, such as game progress, session state, scores, and settings, so that the App can provide features such as save/resume and rewind. This local game data is not submitted to Arcbyte Studio solely because it is stored on your device.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed through the services described above may be used to:

  • Provide and operate Nucleus and its features.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Understand app usage and improve gameplay and performance.
  • Detect, investigate, and fix crashes, bugs, and technical problems.
  • Provide Google Play Games functionality when you choose to use it.
  • Protect the App, users, and services against fraud, abuse, and security issues.

3. Service Providers and Sharing

We use third-party service providers and platform services, including Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Google Play Games, as applicable to the features you use. These providers may process information on our behalf or for their own services in accordance with their respective policies.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is removed by you, the App is uninstalled, or the relevant data is otherwise cleared by the operating system or App.

Information processed by third-party services is retained according to the applicable service provider's retention policies and settings. For information about Google's handling of data, please review Google's privacy documentation.

5. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the App. Third-party services used by the App may also apply their own security controls. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Nucleus is not directed specifically to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly request or collect personal information from children through a Nucleus account.

7. Your Choices

You can control certain advertising and privacy settings through your Android device and Google account settings. You can also choose whether to use Google Play Games features.

8. Third-Party Policies

Third-party services used by Nucleus have their own privacy policies and terms. For example, Google's services are governed by Google's applicable privacy practices. We encourage you to review the policies of services you choose to use.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its services, or applicable legal requirements change. The updated policy will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
